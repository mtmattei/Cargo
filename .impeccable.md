# Design Context: Westhaven Dispatch (Cargo)

Source: the product owner's own words across the 2026-09-29/30 design sessions.

## Audience
Port operations dispatchers working a live shift (berths, cargo, river traffic, security) on a large desktop display. They scan status at a glance and act on exceptions; they return to the same screens all shift.

## Use cases
- Know what is happening in the harbour right now (Overview).
- Plan and assign berths, work the vessel alongside (Berths).
- Find a container, follow it, inspect holds (Cargo).
- Coordinate the river fleet and lockings (Waterways).
- Zones, access and inspections (Security).

## Brand personality / tone
- "Professional operation app", explicitly not "a children's toy". Isometric pastel was rejected.
- The harbour is a "real-life miniature": true scale, tilt-shift, studio light, muted colour; colour is reserved for status.
- Every page follows the Overview: "uniform and cohesive UI/UX". Flat ruled sections, hairlines, ink status words; no boxed-card clutter.
- Modern and restrained. No costume or period metaphors.

## Banned (owner's "AI slop" list)
Coloured or highlighted left borders on containers, glossy gradients, generic 3D, mascots, TLDR boxes, decorative flourishes.

## Feedback protocol
The owner gives numbers ("radius 16px", "15% faster"); respond with numbers.
